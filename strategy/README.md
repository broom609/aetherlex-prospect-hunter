## TL;DR recommendations
- **Best starter niche:** water damage / restoration (or junk removal for easy-mode).
- **Best structure:** service-silo × location, with exact-match `/[service]-[city]-[state]/` money pages.
- **Best first monetization:** pay-per-lead, exclusive, with a free-trial-leads
  offer to remove buyer risk → migrate winners to retainer / rank-and-rent.
- **The asset goal:** ranked sites you rent out — one site = one recurring rent check.

## Important
This kit favors the **legitimate, compliant** path (real entity, real reviews,
truthful content, consent-based tracking) because that path is also what ranks
best and earns AI-engine citations. See **Risk & Compliance** in the master doc
before launch. General guidance, not legal advice — verify state-specific
call-recording, TCPA, and advertising rules with a lawyer.
