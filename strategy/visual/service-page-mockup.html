What is water damage restoration?

Water damage restoration is the process of removing water, drying the structure, and repairing a property after a leak, flood, or burst pipe. It includes inspection, water extraction, dehumidification, antimicrobial treatment, and rebuilding — and should begin within 24–48 hours to prevent mold and structural damage.
