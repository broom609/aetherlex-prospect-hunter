What does an elevator maintenance company do?

An elevator maintenance company provides scheduled preventive maintenance, emergency repairs, safety testing, and equipment modernization to keep building elevators running safely and in compliance with the ASME A17.1 safety code. Services include lubrication and adjustment, parts replacement, 24/7 entrapment response, and support for state-required Category 1 and Category 5 inspections.
