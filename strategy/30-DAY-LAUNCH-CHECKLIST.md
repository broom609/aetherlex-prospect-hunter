# 30-Day Launch Checklist

A day-by-day execution plan to go from zero to first ranked pages + first paying
lead buyer. Check boxes as you go. Assumes ~2–4 focused hours/day.

---

## Week 1 — Validate & lay the foundation

**Day 1 — Niche & metro**
- [ ] Pick niche (start: water damage restoration, or junk removal for easy mode).
- [ ] Pick metro hub + list 15–25 candidate suburbs/towns.

**Day 2 — Market scoring**
- [ ] Pull median home value, income, homeownership %, population per town (Census).
- [ ] Score each town on the §2 framework.

**Day 3 — Competition audit**
- [ ] Manual SERP + map-pack audit per town (directories ranking = opportunity).
- [ ] Choose **1 anchor city + 5–10 tail cities**.

**Day 4 — Brand & legal**
- [ ] Choose brand name + domain (keyword or brandable, .com).
- [ ] Form LLC / register entity; get a real answerable phone number.
- [ ] Draft NAP (name/address/phone) — keep consistent everywhere.

**Day 5 — Infrastructure**
- [ ] Stand up hosting/CMS (GoHighLevel agency OR WordPress+GeneratePress on Cloudways).
- [ ] Install GA4, Search Console, Microsoft Clarity.
- [ ] Set up call tracking (CallRail / GHL) with recording disclosure per state.

**Day 6 — Data layer**
- [ ] Build the per-city data spreadsheet (neighborhoods, ZIPs, landmarks, local
      risks, price range, response time, 2 unique notes per city).

**Day 7 — Templates & assets**
- [ ] Finalize homepage/service/city/FAQ templates + schema blocks.
- [ ] Gather logo, real photos, certifications, initial reviews.

---

## Week 2 — Build the core (first 20 pages)

**Day 8–9 — Money pages foundation**
- [ ] Homepage (full wireframe + Organization/LocalBusiness/WebSite/FAQ schema).
- [ ] Services hub + primary service page.

**Day 10–11 — City money pages**
- [ ] Anchor-city money page + 4 more city money pages (unique local content each).
- [ ] Wire breadcrumb + LocalBusiness + Service + FAQ schema.

**Day 12 — Conversion pages**
- [ ] Get-a-Quote page + multi-step lead form + click-to-call + thank-you page.
- [ ] Missed-call text-back live.

**Day 13 — Trust pages**
- [ ] About (E-E-A-T), Reviews (real reviews + schema), Contact, FAQ hub.
- [ ] Privacy policy, terms, call-recording & SMS consent notices.

**Day 14 — Content + index foundation**
- [ ] 2 resource posts (cost guide + "what to do") + 1 comparison page.
- [ ] Internal linking pass (silo links, footer city/service links).
- [ ] XML sitemap + robots.txt (allow AI crawlers); submit to GSC; request indexing.
- [ ] **Gate check:** 20 pages live, schema validates, forms/calls fire test leads.

---

## Week 3 — Index, expand, prove demand

**Day 15–16 — Local presence**
- [ ] If operating a real entity: create + verify Google Business Profile (no fakes).
      Otherwise plan to use the buyer's real GBP for the map pack.
- [ ] Start core local citations with identical NAP (data aggregators + top dirs).

**Day 17–19 — Expand pages**
- [ ] Publish 5–10 more city/resource pages (staggered, unique, human-edited).
- [ ] Add FAQ blocks + key-takeaways boxes to all money pages (AEO/GEO).

**Day 20 — Tracking live**
- [ ] Rank tracker for money keywords + Local Falcon grid baseline.
- [ ] Looker Studio dashboard connected (GA4, GSC, CallRail, leads sheet).

**Day 21 — Automation end-to-end test**
- [ ] Run test leads through intake→score→route→notify→CRM→billing.
- [ ] **Gate check:** pages indexed, impressions in GSC, automation works.

---

## Week 4 — First leads & first buyer

**Day 22–23 — Prime lead flow**
- [ ] Optional: small Google LSA / local PPC to seed initial leads for the pitch.
- [ ] Confirm GBP + citations + on-page are pulling early calls/forms.

**Day 24 — Build the buffer & buyer list**
- [ ] Collect 3–5 real qualified leads.
- [ ] Pull 30–40 buyer prospects from Google Maps (prioritize those paying for leads).
- [ ] Record a 2-minute Loom audit of the local SERP + your site + a real lead.

**Day 25–28 — Outreach**
- [ ] Run the multi-touch sequence (email → call → VM+SMS → Loom) to 20–40 buyers.
- [ ] Offer **first 5 leads free, exclusive, no contract**.
- [ ] Sign the first buyer; set up instant delivery (SMS+email+CRM).

**Day 29 — Deliver & prove**
- [ ] Deliver leads in real time with call recordings; capture outcomes.
- [ ] Collect a testimonial + ROI numbers.

**Day 30 — Convert & plan scale**
- [ ] Convert buyer from free trial to **$X/qualified lead** (or retainer).
- [ ] Review dashboard; pick next 5–10 cities; queue next niche/market.
- [ ] **Gate check:** ≥1 paying buyer + ≥1 delivered lead + ranking movement.

---

## Success gates
- **Day 14:** 20 pages live & indexed; schema valid; lead flow tested.
- **Day 21:** impressions in GSC; automation end-to-end working.
- **Day 30:** ≥1 buyer on a paid arrangement; ≥1 real lead delivered; early rankings.

## Repeat to scale
Once one city+niche proves out: clone the template, swap the data layer, expand
cities, then replicate the whole asset into a new metro or niche. The end-state per
market is **rank-and-rent** (one ranked site = one monthly rent check).
