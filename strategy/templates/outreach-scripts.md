# Outreach Scripts — Selling Qualified Leads

**Core frame:** you sell **customers / booked jobs**, not "marketing." Lead with the
result and reverse the risk. All scripts: `[Service]`, `[City]`, `[Brand]`,
`[YourName]`, `[X]` = per-lead price.

> Compliance: cold email must follow CAN-SPAM (identify yourself, physical address,
> opt-out). Cold SMS to non-opted-in numbers risks TCPA — prefer SMS only after a
> call/voicemail or to business numbers, and check state rules (Master §15).

---

## 1. Cold email (primary, scalable)

**Subject lines (test these):**
- `[service] customers in [City]`
- `quick question, [FirstName]`
- `[City] homeowner needs [service]`

**Body:**
```
Hi [FirstName],

I run a website that ranks for "[service] in [City]" and I'm getting calls and
form requests from local homeowners who need [service] right now.

I'm not looking to sell you marketing — I just have more leads than I can place.
I'd rather send them to one good local company than to the directories.

Want me to send you the next few for free so you can judge the quality? If they
turn into jobs, we can talk about an ongoing arrangement. No contract.

[YourName]
[Brand] · [phone] · [address]
Reply STOP to opt out.
```
Keep it under 90 words. One CTA. Follow up 3–4× over 2 weeks if no reply.

---

## 2. Cold call (highest conversion for local trades)

```
"Hi, is this [Owner]? — Great. My name's [YourName], I'll be quick.

I run a [service] website that ranks in [City], and I've got homeowners calling
in who need [service]. I'm not selling marketing — I literally have leads I need
to place with a good local company.

Are you guys taking on new [service] jobs in [City] right now?

[Yes] Perfect. Here's what I'll do — I'll send you the next few leads free so you
can see they're real and they close. If you like them, it's $[X] per qualified
lead after that, exclusive to you, no contract. Fair?

What's the best number and email to send a lead to the second it comes in?"
```
**Objection — "How much?"** → *"First few are free. After that $[X] per qualified
lead — and a [service] job is worth $[job value], so it pays for itself on the
first close."*
**Objection — "I'm too busy / booked."** → *"Even better — I'll hold them or send
only the emergency ones. Want just the urgent jobs?"*

---

## 3. Voicemail (when no answer — drive a callback)

```
"Hi [Owner], it's [YourName]. I've got [service] leads coming in from homeowners
in [City] and I'm looking for one company to send them to — free to start.
Call me back at [phone]. Again, that's [phone]. Thanks."
```
Keep under 15 seconds. Follow immediately with the SMS below.

---

## 4. SMS (after call/voicemail)

```
Hi [Owner], [YourName] here — I left a VM. I've got [service] leads in [City]
I want to send you (first few free). Worth a quick chat? Reply YES and I'll send
the next one. Reply STOP to opt out.
```

---

## 5. Loom audit (the differentiator — record your screen, 2–3 min)

**Outline:**
1. *"Hey [Owner], made you a quick 2-minute video."*
2. Google `[service] [City]` live: *"Here's what shows up — see how it's mostly
   directories and your competitors? You're not on page one for this."*
3. Show your ranking site + the map pack: *"This site I run ranks here, and it's
   capturing calls from people searching right now."*
4. Show a (redacted) real lead/call: *"Here's an actual [City] lead that came in
   yesterday."*
5. CTA: *"I'd rather send these to you than the big directories. I'll send the
   next few free — reply and I'll set it up."*

Send the Loom link in the follow-up email/SMS. This converts best because it's
proof, personalized, and shows you already have the asset.

---

## 6. In-person / drop-by (high-ticket trades)

```
"Hey, are you the owner? I'll be 30 seconds — I run the [service] site that ranks
in [City] and I've got homeowner leads I need to place. I'd rather give them to a
local crew than a call center. Can I send you the next few free to try?"
```
Leave a one-pager: what you do, "first 5 leads free, exclusive, no contract,"
your phone/email, and one real stat (e.g., "X leads delivered in [City] last month").

---

## Targeting & cadence
- Pull 30–50 buyers from Google Maps (`[service] [City]`). Prioritize those already
  paying for leads (Google Ads, LSA badge, Angi/Thumbtack) and the hungry #4–#15,
  not the #1 player.
- Cadence per prospect (~2 weeks): Email → Call → VM+SMS → Loom → Call → Email →
  Drop-by (for top targets).
- Goal: pitch 20–40 to land the first 1–3 buyers.

## The first-10-leads play
1. Build a buffer of 3–5 real qualified leads.
2. Offer **first 5 free, exclusive, no contract** to the best-fit buyer.
3. Deliver instantly with call recording/transcript as proof.
4. After they close a job → convert to **$[X]/qualified lead**.
5. Capture the outcome (job won? value?) → use the ROI to raise price + recruit the
   next buyer, then move winners to retainer/rank-and-rent.
